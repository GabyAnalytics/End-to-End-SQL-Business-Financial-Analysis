# Business Questions

## Revenue & Sales

1. What is the company's overall sales performance?
2. How does revenue change over time?
3. Which products generate the most revenue?
4. Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
5. Which customers generate the highest revenue?

## Customer Performance

6. Which customers are high-value customers?
7. How frequently do customers purchase?
8. What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
9. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?

## Returns & Revenue Leakage

10. Which products have the highest return rates?
11. Which categories are responsible for the largest value of returned products?
12. How much potential revenue is lost through returns?
13. Are there products that generate high sales but also unusually high returns?

## Payments

14. What payment methods are most commonly used?
15. Are there unusual payment patterns or payment-related issues?

## Management Decision Questions

16. Which products should management prioritize?
17. Which products/categories may require investigation?
18. Which customers should receive greater retention attention?
19. Where are the biggest opportunities to improve financial performance?
